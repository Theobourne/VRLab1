Lab 2

Textures (Poly Haven, CC0):
 - Floor: Raw Wooden Floor 01 - https://polyhaven.com/a/raw_wooden_floor_01
 - Walls: Rock Wall 17 - https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_17
   (normal and roughness maps converted from EXR to PNG for Godot)

No asset packages. The toy box, table marker, ceiling, lights and music
were made in Godot (boxes, noise textures and a generated tone).
